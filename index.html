Austin J. Stromme

I am an assistant professor in the Statistics Department at ENSAE/CREST. I work at the high-dimensional intersection of optimization, geometry, and probability, and am particularly interested in the effects of symmetries and over-parameterization on learning dynamics and generalization. Previously, I received my PhD from MIT EECS.
